## The problem
The Rwandan Community of Maine needed to run its 2026 community election with members living across the state. In-person voting would have left people out, and any tool had to work in both Kinyarwanda and English.

## What we built
A secure online voting platform where each eligible member could cast one ballot in their preferred language.

## How it works
Each eligible member gets access to one ballot. They choose Kinyarwanda or English, read the same candidates and questions in that language, and cast their vote from wherever they are in Maine. Once a member has voted, that ballot is closed.

The whole election runs in one place online, so members didn't have to travel to vote and organizers didn't have to run polling sites across the state. This project was about getting the basics exactly right: one member, one ballot, in their language.

## Results
Built in a few days, the platform let voters across Maine cast ballots online in both languages. After the election, the community asked AImbase to build its website.

## What we learned
Language is access. Offering the ballot in Kinyarwanda as well as English mattered as much as putting it online. Speed mattered too: a community election has a date, and software that's ready a week late is no help at all.
