## The problem
After the election, the community wanted a permanent online home and a reliable way to collect recurring member contributions and donations.

## What we built
A community website that gives members and neighbors one place to find the organization, with recurring member contributions and one-time donations built into the site.

## How it works
A member picks an amount and a schedule once, and their contribution runs on its own after that. Anyone can also give a one-time donation from the same site. The goal is simple: supporting the community should take a minute, not a reminder every month.

## Results
The website continues the work that started with the election: the community asked for it because the first project worked. It gives the organization one address to share and a steady way to fund its work.

## What we learned
When a community trusts you with its vote, the next project is about keeping that trust. Recurring contributions only work if members feel as confident giving as they did voting.
